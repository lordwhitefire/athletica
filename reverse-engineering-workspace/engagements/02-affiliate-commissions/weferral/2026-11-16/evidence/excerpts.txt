EVIDENCE 2 — CreateCampaign state: campaign-level commission fields
Source: samples/weferral/app/routes/Forms/CreateCampaign/CreateCampaign.js
Proves: campaign carries BOTH reward_type AND commission_type (decoupled).
        Fields on the campaign object:
          reward_type: 'cash_reward' | 'discount_coupon'
          commission_type: (selected when reward_type === 'cash_reward')
          reward: the percentage OR fixed amount (depending on commission_type)
          recurring: bool (enable recurring commission)
          recurring_limit: int (max # transactions rewarded, e.g. 12 = 12 months)
          minimum_cash_payout: number (threshold below which no payout)
          payout_term: (net terms, e.g. net-30)
        This is RICHER than Raider's single account.commission BigDecimal.

EVIDENCE 4 — Locale_Base URL construction rules (the ASIN → URL brain)
Source: samples/amazon-auto-links/include/core/_common/utility/locale/_abstract/AmazonAutoLinks_Locale_Base.php:120-210
Proves: the URL patterns for every Amazon surface:
        - Product detail:  https://{domain}/dp/{ASIN}                    (NOT /gp/product/ — /dp/ is current)
        - Customer review: https://{domain}/product-reviews/{ASIN}?tag={id}&language={lang}
        - Best sellers:    https://{domain}/gp/bestsellers/{path}
        - Add to cart:      https://{domain}/gp/aws/cart/add.html
        - Rating widget:    https://{domain}/gp/customer-reviews/widgets/average-customer-review/popover/ref=dpx_acr_pop_?contextId=dpx&asin={ASIN}
        - Associates URL:   per-locale sAssociatesURL field (e.g. affiliate-program.amazon.com)
        - No-image:         https://images-na.ssl-images-amazon.com/images/G/{localeNumber}/x-site/icons/no-img-sm.gif
        Rule: ONE class knows how to build every Amazon URL for its marketplace.

EVIDENCE 6 — product schema has expiration_time + error column (TTL-based refresh)
Source: samples/amazon-auto-links/include/core/_common/utility/database/AmazonAutoLinks_DatabaseTable_aal_products.php:30-120
Proves: aal_products table stores: asin, locale, modified_time, expiration_time,
        title, price, currency, rating, is_prime, is_adult, error (string), etc.
        KEY RULE: expiration_time column = products auto-expire and must be
        re-fetched from PAAPI. The 'error' column records fetch failures.
        This is the closest thing to a 'dead-link' detector: a product with
        expiration_time < now() is stale and is re-queried; a product whose PAAPI
        fetch returned an error gets the error string stored.

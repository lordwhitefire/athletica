# Plan — Rollout as the MVP

**Date:** 2026-10-24
**Status:** Awaiting the owner's go

## What the owner asked for

A recommendation on the `rollout` app as the MVP: what it must include to launch fast, what it must not include, and how to tell whether it worked.

## What I will produce

One recommendation in `product-management/` covering:

- who the MVP is for;
- the underlying need it serves;
- the MVP scope — what is in and what is out;
- the options considered and what the chosen one gives up;
- one testable success metric;
- the reasoning and sources, including what in the repo supports each claim.

## Method

The question is worked through my seven-step method, CIRCLES, using the skill that holds each step. The steps run in order; none is skipped or merged.

## Inputs

- `athletica/rollout/` — the app as it exists now, read-only.
- `athletica/openspec/changes/roll-arch/` — the two-app plan and its tasks.
- `athletica/openspec/changes/athletica-architecture/` — the target architecture.
- The owner's answers to what the repo cannot answer: who the first users are, what "fast" means in practice, and where those first users will come from.

## Boundaries

- I do not write code, change configuration, deploy, or create tickets.
- I write only inside `product-management/`.
- The recommendation is advice. The decision remains the owner's.

## Sequence

1. Comprehend — establish the goal, constraints, and context.
2. Identify — settle the customer and the stakeholder.
3. Report — name the underlying need behind the stated want.
4. Cut — choose the need that wins and state what is given up.
5. List — produce genuinely different MVP options.
6. Evaluate — weigh them against what matters now.
7. Summarize — one recommendation, one metric, and the reasoning.

## Stop point

The work ends when the recommendation is handed back. Nothing is built, shipped, or changed by me.

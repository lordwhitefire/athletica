## 4. New Balance — PDP Related Product Sections

### 4.1 Section Titles Used
- **"Complete the Look"** — Outfit/styling recommendations (shoes + apparel). Product images in a grid.
- **"Goes Well With"** — Complementary products positioned below main product info.
- **"You May Also Like"** (inferred from industry standard) — General recommendations.
- **"Recently Viewed"** (inferred) — Footer browsing history.
- **"Shop all {Category}"** — Links to full category page.

*Source: Direct observation of NewBalance.com (530 product page MR530-32265-PMG-NA).*

### 4.2 Number of Products Per Section
- "Complete the Look": ~4 products in a grid.
- "Goes Well With": 4–6 products.

### 4.3 Presentation Format
**Desktop:** "Complete the Look" — grid of styled product images. "Goes Well With" — horizontal row of cards with image, name, price, color variants.

**Mobile:** Vertical stacks or horizontal scroll rows. Simplified cards.

### 4.4 Vertical Positioning
1. Breadcrumb → 2. Gallery + Info panel (size/width selectors) → 3. **"Goes Well With"** (below Add to Bag, in product info area) → 4. **"Complete the Look"** → 5. Product description/features → 6. Fit guidance → 7. Reviews → 8. Footer

### 4.5 Selection Logic
1. **Category Affinity** — Same product category.
2. **Collection Matching** — Same numbered series (e.g., 530 collection).
3. **Color Coordination** — "Goes Well With" uses color info.
4. **Lifestyle Segment** — Keeps performance vs lifestyle separate.
5. **Manual Merchandising** — New arrivals/collections boosted.
6. **Explicit exclusion of current product.**

### 4.6 Notable UX Patterns
1. "Goes Well With" in product info area — highly visible without scrolling.
2. "Complete the Look" with styled imagery.
3. Inline fit guidance ("Most People Go Down 0.5 US Sizes") — unique among researched sites.
4. Width-based sizing prominently featured.
5. Afterpay BNPL integration on PDP.
6. Color swatches on product cards.
7. Clean, minimalist aesthetic.

### 4.7 Mobile vs Desktop
- Desktop: sidebar/inline cross-sells, multi-column grids.
- Mobile: full-width stacked sections, sticky "Add to Bag" CTA.

*Source: Direct observation of NewBalance.com (530 PDP).*

## 5. Reebok — PDP Related Product Sections

### 5.1 Section Titles Used
- **"Complete the Look"** — Outfit-building cross-sell (auto-styles shoes + accessories + apparel).
- **"You May Also Like"** — General product recommendations.
- **"Recently Viewed"** — Footer + checkout browsing history.
- **"Trending Now"** — Popular items on category/collection pages.
- **"Bundle & Save"** — Promotional bundle pricing.

*Source: RevLifter blog (2023-08-11); SqueezeGrowth "20 Best Product Cross-Sell Examples" (2025-10-18); Reebok.com observation.*

### 5.2 Number of Products Per Section
- "Complete the Look": 2–4 products forming an outfit.
- "You May Also Like": 4–8.
- "Recently Viewed": 4–10.

### 5.3 Presentation Format
**Desktop:** "Complete the Look" — curated set forming full outfit. "You May Also Like" — horizontal carousel with arrows. Cards show image, name, price, color variants, badges. "Recently Viewed" at checkout is a subtle row.

**Mobile:** Horizontal scroll rows, touch/swipe, full-width cards, sticky CTA.

### 5.4 Vertical Positioning
1. Banner → 2. Breadcrumb → 3. Gallery + Info panel → 4. **"Complete the Look"** (below Add to Cart, above details) → 5. Product details → 6. Reviews → 7. **"You May Also Like"** → 8. **"Recently Viewed"** → 9. Footer

### 5.5 Selection Logic
1. **Product Tagging / "Complete the Look"** — Shopify-based tagging for complementary items (rule-based, manually curated).
2. **Category Affinity** — Same sport/category.
3. **Collaborative Filtering** — "You May Also Like."
4. **Browsing History** — "Recently Viewed."
5. **Manual Merchandising** — Collaborations/special collections boosted.
6. **Price Range Matching** — Same price band.

### 5.6 Notable UX Patterns
1. "Complete the Look" auto-styling — assembles full outfits, reducing decision fatigue.
2. "Recently Viewed" at checkout — gentle reminder without being pushy.
3. "Bundle & Save" promotions.
4. Discount badges on product cards (clear % off).
5. Color swatches on recommendation cards.
6. Free shipping progress bar.

### 5.7 Mobile vs Desktop
- Desktop: inline cross-sells, full-width carousels.
- Mobile: stacked sections, swipe carousels, sticky CTA.
- "Complete the Look" becomes compact swipeable row.

*Source: SqueezeGrowth (2025-10-18); RevLifter (2023-08-11); Reebok.com observation.*

## 7. Key Patterns Across All Sites

1. **"Complete the Look" is Universal** — Every site uses some version. Consistent naming across Puma, Foot Locker, New Balance, Reebok.
2. **"You May Also Like" is Standard** — All sites use this as primary algorithmic rec section.
3. **Horizontal Carousels Dominate** — All five use horizontal scroll carousels for related products.
4. **Vertical Positioning is Consistent** — Product info → Outfit cross-sell → Product details → Reviews → Algorithmic recs → Recently Viewed → Footer.
5. **Ratings on Recommendation Cards** — Most include star ratings for social proof.
6. **Color Swatches Nearly Universal** — 4 of 5 sites show color variants on rec cards.
7. **Same-Brand Only** — No cross-brand recommendations.
8. **Recently Viewed in Footer** — Universal pattern.

*Source: Compiled from all sources cited above.*

## 8. Recommendations for Athletica (Football Boot PDP)

### Section Titles
- **"You Might Also Like"** — General algorithmic recs (industry standard).
- **"Complete the Kit"** — Football-specific twist on "Complete the Look" (boots → socks, shin guards, training wear).
- **"Frequently Bought Together"** — Bundling (boots + bag + socks + care kit).
- **"More from {Brand}"** — e.g., "More from Nike", "More from Adidas".
- **"Recently Viewed"** — Footer section.

### Presentation
- **Horizontal scroll-snap carousel** (CSS `scroll-snap-type: x mandatory`) — lightweight, native, smooth.
- **4 cards visible on desktop**, 1.5–2 on mobile.
- **Left/right arrow buttons** on hover (desktop), touch swipe (mobile).
- **Product card:** Image, name, price, color swatches, traction type badge (FG, SG, AG, TF), brand logo.
- **Ratings on cards** for social proof.
- **"Add all to cart"** for "Frequently Bought Together" bundles.

### Vertical Positioning
1. Product info (sizes, colors, Add to Cart)
2. **"Complete the Kit"** — High position, after product info, before reviews — shows complementary items (socks, shin guards, training wear, bags)
3. Product description/details
4. Customer Reviews
5. **"You Might Also Like"** — Below reviews
6. **"Frequently Bought Together"** — Below that
7. **"More from {Brand}"** — Below that
8. **"Recently Viewed"** — Footer

### Selection Logic
- Same traction type (FG → other FG boots)
- Same brand (Nike → other Nike boots)
- Same price tier (±30%)
- Same sport (football only)
- Explicit exclusion of current product
- Manual override for new releases and featured products
- Co-purchase data for "Frequently Bought Together"
- "Complete the Kit": boots + socks + shin guards + bag

### Mobile Considerations
- Sticky "Add to Cart" CTA at bottom.
- Carousels use native touch swipe.
- Full-width product cards.
- Sections stack vertically.

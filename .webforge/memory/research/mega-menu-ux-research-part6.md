## Section 4 (continued): Athletica-Specific Recommendations

### 4.5 Unresolved Items [NOT FOUND — ask CEO]

The following items were not definitively resolved by the research and require CEO/Product Owner input:

1. **Audience segmentation:** Should the top-level nav split by Gender (Men's/Women's/Kids') or by Category (Boots/Clothing/Equipment)? Both approaches are validated by competitors (Nike = audience-first, JD Sports = category-first).

2. **Athletica's exact category taxonomy:** The exact Level 1 categories (Boots, Clothing, Equipment, Accessories — or different) need to be confirmed for the final taxonomy.

3. **Surface types:** Is "By Surface" (FG/AG/TF/SG) the correct second-dimension filter? Are there other surfaces or attributes that should be first-class navigation dimensions?

4. **Brand list:** Which brands will Athletica carry? This affects the Column 2 brand listing in the mega menu.

5. **Mobile search persistence:** Should the search icon remain in the header after the hamburger menu opens? Nike does this; Decathlon puts search above categories.

6. **Cookie-based personalization:** Should Athletica implement Zalando's cookie-based gender/segment preference to skip the initial segmentation step for returning visitors?

7. **Promotional column ownership:** Who manages the Column 4 promotional content? Marketing team? This affects whether the pattern is feasible long-term.

## Section 7: Other Sports/Fashion Sites

*Sources: `pdp-related-products-other-sports.md` and `pdp-related-products-other-sports-part2.md` (Probe-Sable, 2026-07-18)*

### 7.1 Puma.com

**Section Titles Used:**
- **"How to Wear It"** — Styling tool showing product in multiple outfits (powered by Stylitics AI).
- **"You May Also Like"** — Algorithmic general recommendations.
- **"Frequently Bought Together"** — Bundling suggestions based on co-purchase data.
- **"Shop the Model" / "Shop the Look"** — Links products worn by model in hero image.
- **"Complete the Look"** — Curated complementary products. Dedicated page at `/collections/lifestyle/complete-the-look`.
- **"Recently Viewed"** — Footer-level browsing history.

*Sources: Stylitics blog (2025-08-13); Retail TouchPoints (2024-01-08); Puma.com observation*

**Number of Products:** "How to Wear It": 4–6 styled looks, 3–6 products per look. "You May Also Like": ~8–12 loaded, 4 visible on desktop. "Frequently Bought Together": 3–4 products in bundle. "Complete the Look": 4–8 products in carousel.

**Presentation Format:** Desktop — "How to Wear It" is a carousel of styled outfit images (studio photography). Products in each look are clickable. "You May Also Like" — horizontal carousel with arrows. "Frequently Bought Together" — grid row with combined pricing + "Add all to cart." Mobile — Same sections, full-width carousels, touch swipe. Sticky "Add to Bag" CTA.

**Vertical Positioning:** Breadcrumb → Gallery + Info panel → **"How to Wear It"** (high, below CTA) → Product details → **"You May Also Like"** → **"Frequently Bought Together"** → Reviews → Recently Viewed → Footer

**Selection Logic:**
1. **AI-Powered Styling (Stylitics)** — Automatically outfits products with complementary items considering style, color, trends, inventory.
2. **Collaborative Filtering** — "You May Also Like" from co-viewing/co-purchase.
3. **Co-Purchase Data** — "Frequently Bought Together" from transactions.
4. **Manual Merchandising** — Can boost specific items to front of carousels.
5. **Shop the Model** — Exact product matching from hero image.
6. **Post-Purchase Personalization** — Outfit recs in post-purchase emails.

**Notable UX:** "How to Wear It" styling tool is unique — shows product in multiple fully-styled outfits. "Shop the Model" shows exact products from hero image. AI-driven inventory management — sold-out items auto-replaced. Reported: +3% AOV, +235% conversion, +334% session duration among Stylitics users. *Source: Retail TouchPoints (2024-01-08)*

### 7.2 Decathlon.com

**Section Titles Used:**
- **"You might also like"** — Alternative/similar products.
- **"Customers also viewed"** — Social proof-based recs.
- **"Complete your gear"** — Complementary equipment for same sport.
- **"Frequently bought together"** — Bundling suggestions.
- **"Recently viewed"** — Footer browsing history.
- **"Related products"** — Generic category-based recs.

*Sources: Direct observation of Decathlon.com; Insider case study (2024-09-16)*

**Number of Products:** "You might also like": 4–8. "Customers also viewed": 4–6. "Frequently bought together": 3–4. "Complete your gear": 4–8.

**Presentation Format:** Desktop — Horizontal carousel with arrows. Cards show image, name, price, star rating, review count. "Frequently bought together" includes combined pricing + "Add all to cart." Mobile — Single-column scroll rows, touch swipe, full-width cards.

**Vertical Positioning:** Banner → Breadcrumb → Gallery + Info → Short description → **"You might also like"** (high) → Product details → Reviews → **"Frequently bought together"** → Recently Viewed → Footer

**Selection Logic:**
1. **Category-Based** — Products from same sport (dominant logic).
2. **Co-Viewing / Co-Purchase** — "Customers also viewed" / "Frequently bought together."
3. **In-House Brand Cross-Selling** — Stays within same passion brand (Quechua, Forclaz, Kiprun).
4. **Insider Smart Recommender** — Micro-segmentation, dynamic attributes, behavioral data.
5. **Manual Merchandising** — Seasonal boosts.
6. **Price Range Affinity** — Same price tier.

**Notable UX:** Clean, functional cards with ratings for social proof. Bundle pricing with one-click "Add all to cart." Heavily favors in-house brands. Insider personalization: 8% AOV increase via web push, 14% via checkout optimization. *Source: Insider case study (2024-09-16)*

**Vertical Positioning:** Banner → Breadcrumb → Gallery + Info panel → Shipping info → Product details → **"Complete the Look"** → **"Customers Also Viewed"** → Reviews → **"You May Also Like"** → Recently Viewed → Footer

1. **Brand Affinity** — Nike → more Nike, Jordan → more Jordan.

**Notable UX:** Mobile PDP ranked top 1% by Baymard (Dec 2024). "More Colors Available" with direct links. Size out-of-stock "Notify Me" interface. "Pick Up in Store" option. FLX Rewards integration with member pricing. Color swatches on rec cards.

**Notable UX:** "Goes Well With" in product info area — highly visible without scrolling. "Complete the Look" with styled imagery. ## Section 8: Common Patterns Across All Sites

*Synthesized from all 8 source files across 12 sites (Nike, Adidas, ASOS, Zalando, Mr Porter, Farfetch, Amazon, Puma, Decathlon, Foot Locker, New Balance, Reebok)*

### 8.1 Section Title Patterns

**Most Common Section Titles (by frequency):**
- **"You Might Also Like" / "You May Also Like"** — Used by Nike, Adidas, ASOS, Zalando, Mr Porter, Farfetch, Puma, Foot Locker, Reebok. The universal standard.
- **"Complete the Look"** — Used by Nike, Zalando, Farfetch, Puma, Foot Locker, New Balance, Reebok. Nearly universal for fashion/sport.
- **"Recently Viewed"** — Used by ALL sites. Universal footer pattern.
- **"Frequently Bought Together"** — Used by Amazon, Puma, Decathlon.
- **"Customers Also Viewed"** — Used by Zalando, Decathlon, Foot Locker.

**Title Variations by Retail Tier:**
- **Mass market (Amazon):** "Customers Who Bought This Item Also Bought", "Frequently Bought Together" — data-driven, authoritative.
- **Fashion (ASOS, Zalando):** "Complete the Look", "Similar Items", "You Might Also Like" — suggestive, inspirational.
- **Premium (Mr Porter, Farfetch):** "You Might Also Like...", "More from this Designer" — soft, curated language.
- **Sports (Puma, Foot Locker, Decathlon):** "Complete the Look", "You May Also Like", "Complete your gear" — action-oriented.

### 8.2 Carousel vs Grid vs Bundle Formats

**Horizontal Carousel** — Dominant format across ALL sites for "You Might Also Like" and "Similar Items". Nike/Adidas use CSS scroll-snap. Typical: 4 visible on desktop, 1.5–2 on mobile. Arrow buttons + swipe.

**Bundle Layout (with "Add All to Cart")** — Amazon ("Frequently Bought Together"), Puma, Decathlon. Items connected by "+" icons, total price, single CTA. Proven AOV driver.

**Inline Grid (compact)** — ASOS ("People also bought" — 1×3 grid). For co-purchase data with small product counts.

**Spotlight/Bento Layout** — Farfetch ("Complete the Look"). Hero product + 3–4 recs in cohesive visual group.

**Styled Outfit Images** — Puma ("How to Wear It"), Zalando ("Get the Look"). Full outfit photography with clickable items.

### 8.3 Vertical Positioning Patterns

**Universal order (synthesized from all sites):**
1. Product images + info (above fold)
2. **Outfit/Complementary cross-sell** (high — near Add to Cart area)
3. Product description / details
4. Customer Reviews
5. **Algorithmic recommendations** (below reviews — "You Might Also Like", "Similar Items")
6. **Brand-specific recommendations** ("More from {Brand}")
7. **Recently Viewed** (footer area)
8. Footer

**Key insight:** Outfit/complementary cross-sells consistently appear HIGHER on the page than algorithmic recommendations. Amazon places "Frequently Bought Together" directly below the Buy Box.

### 8.4 Selection Logic Approaches

**Seven distinct approaches observed across all sites:**

1. **Collaborative Filtering** — Nearly all sites for "You Might Also Like" / "Customers Also Bought". Based on co-viewing/co-purchase patterns.
2. **Content-Based Filtering** — ASOS ("SIMILAR ITEMS"), Zalando ("Similar items"). Matches by category, brand, style, colour, price.
3. **Neural Collaborative Filtering (Deep Learning)** — ASOS (TensorFlow 2, Azure ML). Pre-computed embeddings for sub-50ms latency.
4. **AI Style Compatibility** — Zalando (AFC), Puma (Stylitics), Farfetch (FITB). Learns "good outfit" from stylist data.
5. **Attribute-Based Matching** — Nike, Adidas, New Balance. Matches by collection, colour, sport category.
6. **Co-Purchase/Association Rule Mining** — Amazon ("Frequently Bought Together"), Decathlon, Puma.
7. **Manual Merchandising** — All sites for new releases, seasonal pushes, collaborations.

### 8.5 "Complete the Look" Universality

- **Every single fashion/sport site** implements some form of outfit/styling cross-sell.
- Naming varies: "Complete the Look" (Nike, Farfetch, Puma, Foot Locker, New Balance, Reebok, Zalando), "Complete the Set" (Adidas), "Complete your gear" (Decathlon), "How to Wear It" (Puma), "Goes Well With" (New Balance).
- ASOS is the notable exception — no CTL on PDP; uses AI Stylist and Style Match instead.
- **Positioning:** Always high on the page, near the Add to Cart area.
- **Key performance:** Puma reported +3% AOV, +235% conversion, +334% session duration from Stylitics CTL (Retail TouchPoints, 2024).

### 8.6 "You Might Also Like" as Standard

- Used by 9 of 11 sites. Split: "You Might Also Like" (Nike, ASOS, Mr Porter, Farfetch, Zalando) vs "You May Also Like" (Adidas, Puma, Foot Locker, Reebok).
- Always positioned below reviews or below outfit cross-sell.
- Typically 8–16 products, horizontal carousel.
- Uses collaborative filtering + content-based hybrid logic.

### 8.7 Recently Viewed in Footer

- **Every single site** has a "Recently Viewed" section in the footer area.
- Typically 4–10 products, compact horizontal strip.
- Session/cookie tracking sorted by recency.
- Reebok additionally shows "Recently Viewed" at checkout.

### 8.8 Color Swatches on Recommendation Cards

- **Universal pattern across 9 of 11 sites.** Only Decathlon and ASOS lack consistent color swatches on rec cards.
- Used on both desktop and mobile.
- Shows available color variants without clicking through.

### 8.9 Same-Brand-Only Recommendations

- **Every site keeps recommendations within their own brand.** No cross-brand recommendations.
- Exception: Amazon shows "Similar brands on Amazon" as a separate section.
- For multi-brand retailers (ASOS, Zalando, Farfetch), brand is a key attribute in recommendation models.

### 8.10 Mobile Patterns

- **Sticky "Add to Bag" CTA** — Universal across all sites.
- **Swipeable carousels** — All carousels touch-swipeable on mobile.
- **1.5–2 cards visible** per carousel on mobile vs 4 on desktop.
- **Single column layout** — Mobile page collapses to single column.
- **Progressive disclosure** — Accordion sections (Zalando pattern).
- **Full-width product cards** with larger touch targets.

### 8.11 Product Badges on Recommendation Cards

Common badges: "Best Seller" (Nike, Adidas, Amazon), "New"/"Just In" (Nike, Adidas), "-X%" Sale (Adidas, Reebok), "Sustainable Materials" (Nike), "Member Product" (Nike), "Has Matching Item" (Adidas PLP), "Overall Pick"/"Amazon's Choice" (Amazon), "Sponsored" (Amazon, Zalando).

### 8.12 Rating/Review Stars on Rec Cards

- **Present on:** Amazon, Puma, Decathlon, Foot Locker, Reebok.
- **Absent on:** Nike, Adidas (not consistently), Mr Porter, Farfetch (premium sites often omit reviews).
- Mixed evidence — ratings used for social proof on some sites; others keep cards clean.

### Appendix B: Methodology Notes

**Research Methodology:**
- All research was conducted on 2026-07-18 by the Intelligence Team (Probe-Orion, Probe-Wren, Probe-Beacon, Probe-Sable).
- Sources include direct observation of live PDP pages, published articles, academic papers, API documentation, and case studies.
- Selection logic for most commercial sites (Nike, Adidas, Amazon) is inferred from published research and industry patterns, not from direct source code access.
- Exact section titles are quoted where directly observed; otherwise noted as "inferred."
- Mobile observations are based on responsive behavior of desktop sites and mobile-specific UX analysis sources.

**Site Coverage:**
- **Global mass market:** Amazon
- **Sportswear:** Nike, Adidas, Puma, New Balance, Reebok
- **Sports retail:** Foot Locker, Decathlon
- **Fashion e-tail:** ASOS, Zalando
- **Premium fashion:** Mr Porter, Farfetch

**Limitations:**
- Some sites (Nike, Adidas, Amazon) are well-documented; others (New Balance, Reebok) rely more on direct observation.
- Visual search and AI stylist features (ASOS Style Match, ASOS AI Stylist, Zalando Zalon) noted but not analyzed in detail.
- Selection logic for proprietary algorithms (Nike, Adidas) is inferred — actual implementation details are trade secrets.
- Mobile PDP behavior varies by device; observations based on standard mobile viewport assumptions.
